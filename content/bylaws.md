## Article I: Name and Purpose
This organization shall be known as the **Merced de Los Pueblos de Tierra Amarilla**.

## Article II: Meetings and Notice
All regular and special meetings shall strictly adhere to the state **Open Meetings Act**, ensuring proper public notice and transparency for all community members.
